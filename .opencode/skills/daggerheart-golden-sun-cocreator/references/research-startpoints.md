# Research Startpoints

Use these only when the local wiki lacks enough information for the current interview topic. Prefer local wiki knowledge first.

## Golden Sun

- Official/Nintendo pages if available for high-level franchise framing.
- Golden Sun Wiki or equivalent fan wikis for names, locations, Psynergy, Djinn, lighthouses, and plot sequence. Treat fan wiki details as secondary sources and cite them as such.
- Avoid importing long passages. Save only the URL, access date, short summary, and small supporting quotes where useful.

## Daggerheart

- Official Daggerheart SRD, publisher pages, or owned local rule references if available.
- Focus on concepts that affect campaign prep: Hope/Fear, Experiences, domains/classes only if relevant, adversaries, stress, scenes, countdowns/clocks, and collaborative worldbuilding.

## Proactive Roleplaying

- Prefer sources about situations over plots, faction turns, fronts, clocks, active NPC agendas, and player agency.
- Useful search phrases: `proactive roleplaying factions clocks`, `don't prep plots situation-based prep`, `rpg fronts clocks factions player agency`.
- Extract principles, not a whole essay. The interview output should produce usable campaign material.
