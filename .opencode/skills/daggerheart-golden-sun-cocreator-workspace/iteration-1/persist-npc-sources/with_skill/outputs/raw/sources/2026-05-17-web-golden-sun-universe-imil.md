## Kurzsummary

Die Seite beschreibt Imil als Dorf in der winterlichen Nordregion Angaras, nahe beim Mercury Lighthouse. Imil ist mit Mia, Alex, dem alten Mercury Clan und der Rolle der Merkur-Adepten als Hueter des Leuchtturms verbunden. Fuer die Kampagne stuetzt die Quelle Nara als Merkur-Seherin aus dem Umfeld von Imil, deren Motive sich um Heilung, Verantwortung, Schuld, Leuchtturmwissen und die Gefahr des Missbrauchs von Alchemie drehen koennen.

## Fuer die Kampagnenentscheidung relevante Belege

> Imil is located close to Mercury Lighthouse within the winter-locked climes of northern Angara and is home to Mia of the ancient Mercury Clan.

> Imil [...] is a village featured in Golden Sun, located near the wintry northern coast of the northern continent of Angara.

> She and her cousin Alex are descendants of the ancient Mercury Clan of Adepts who once lived near the lighthouse and considered themselves its protectors.

> Because of what is presented in-game as a flu epidemic, the weapon and armor vendors do not sell their wares during this initial visit.

> Mia is going around the village and making use of her healing abilities to help those who are sick.

> After winning the challenging boss battle against Saturos at the aerie of Mercury Lighthouse, the party will be deposited back outside its front entrance, where the fountain in front of the lighthouse will be filled with healing water.

> For many generations the Mercury Clan has been responsible for keeping Mercury Lighthouse from being lit.

> Ultimately Isaac's team, even with Mia's assistance, are unable to scale the lighthouse fast enough to keep it from being lit. Fortunately, with the Mercury Beacon glowing brightly, the fountain at the base of the lighthouse is filled with the Water of Hermes, mystical water that can heal all wounds and diseases.
