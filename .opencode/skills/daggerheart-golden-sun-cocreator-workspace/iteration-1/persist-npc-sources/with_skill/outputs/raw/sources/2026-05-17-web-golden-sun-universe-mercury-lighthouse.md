## Kurzsummary

Die Seite beschreibt den Mercury Lighthouse als Merkur-ausgerichteten Elemental Lighthouse nahe Imil. Er ist ein zentraler Dungeon und Storyort in Golden Sun, verbunden mit Mia, dem Mercury Clan, dem Mercury Star, dem Mercury Beacon, dem Water of Hermes, Ply-Psynergy, Wassermechanik, verborgenen Wegen, Dschinn und der Frage nach der Rueckkehr der Alchemie. Fuer Nara liefert die Quelle Material fuer Seherinnenmotive, Leuchtturmbarrieren, Heilwasser, verborgene Erinnerungen im Wasser und proaktive Konflikte um Zugang, Warnungen und Missbrauch.

## Fuer die Kampagnenentscheidung relevante Belege

> Mercury Lighthouse is located in the wintry northern area of Angara, near Imil.

> Mercury Lighthouse [...] the Mercury-aligned of the Elemental Lighthouses [...] is located in the wintry snow-covered northern region of the northern continent of Angara near the village of Imil, home of the ancient Mercury Clan dedicated to making sure the Lighthouse is never lit.

> Mia permanently joins the party early in Mercury Lighthouse, and after a rather challenging boss battle at the top of the tower, the important Hermes' Water can be collected from the fountain at its base before departing back for Kolima Forest.

> casting the Move Psynergy to move it one tile left will enact a cutscene in which Mia brings up her Ply Psynergy, which she uses to dispel the barrier and enter on her own.

> Mercury Lighthouse's "waterfall walls" sometimes conceal hidden chambers, which you can reach by pressing against them to walk through them.

> Casting the Ply Psynergy while standing on this giant goddess statue will activate the tiles' ability to let you hop across the water itself.

> The Mercury Lighthouse existed in Weyard's ancient past much like the other three Elemental Lighthouses as a source of its respective element in Psynergy and Alchemy.

> The location of the town of Imil was once the home of the Mercury Clan, who were the guardians of Mercury Lighthouse, and the modern-day residents have had legends passed down that the Lighthouse is to remain dormant.

> They also have the legend, however, that if the Lighthouse is lit once again, the empty fountain at its base will be perpetually filled with a mystical healing water referred to as the Water of Hermes, which has the power to stave off all illness and ensure physical health.

> As an Elemental Lighthouse, it may not be entered without the presence of a Mercury Adept with access to the healing Psynergy power of Ply, such as Adepts descended from the Mercury Clan bloodline.

## Nutzung fuer Nara

- Nara kann als NPC funktionieren, deren Merkur-Sehen an Leuchtturmwasser, Barrieren, versteckte Kammern, Heilwasser oder Nachwirkungen des Mercury Beacon gebunden ist.
- Der Leuchtturm liefert proaktive Druckpunkte: Zugang, Wachen, alte Mechanismen, Alchemie, Entfessler, Heilerorden und Angst vor erneuter Eskalation.
- Die Quelle stuetzt eine Auslegung von Merkur-Psynergy, die heilen, oeffnen, erinnern, verbergen und fuehren kann, ohne Nara allwissend zu machen.
